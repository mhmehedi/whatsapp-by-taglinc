Whatsapp by TagLinc — How to install
=====================================

Needs: macOS 14 (Sonoma) or newer. Works on both Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.

1. Drag "Whatsapp by TagLinc" onto the "Applications" folder in this window.

2. Open Whatsapp by TagLinc from Applications.
   macOS will say it "can't be opened" or "could not verify" it,
   because the app is not from the App Store. Click "Done" / "OK".

3. Open System Settings -> Privacy & Security.
   Scroll down. Next to "Whatsapp by TagLinc was blocked", click "Open Anyway",
   then confirm with your Mac password or Touch ID.
   (You only need to do this once.)

   Alternative: open Terminal and run
     xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Whatsapp by TagLinc.app"

4. Scan the QR code with WhatsApp on your phone
   (Settings -> Linked devices -> Link a device).

Using it
--------
  +            add another WhatsApp account (or Cmd+N)
  Cmd+1 ... 9  switch between accounts
  Right-click  an account to rename or remove it
  Cmd+R        reload the current account

Memory
------
  An account that keeps growing (past 700 MB and 400 MB above its size when
  it opened) is reloaded automatically while you're not looking at it.
  It stays logged in.
  Account -> Sleep When Not Used: closes that account after 15 minutes unused,
  so it uses no memory. Click it to open it again. A sleeping account gets no
  notifications.

Run only one copy of the app at a time, from Applications -
not from inside the DMG.
